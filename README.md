# Privacy Policy

Thank you for using **Libgen** ("App", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Libgen mobile application available on Android and iOS.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

# 1. About Libgen

Libgen is a book discovery and search application that allows users to search publicly available book metadata and open third-party webpages for additional information.

The App **does not host book files on its own servers**. When users choose to view more information, they may be redirected to third-party websites that are outside of our control.

# 5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, we comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

Before accessing the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), we request your permission.

You may:

* Allow Tracking
* Deny Tracking

If you deny permission:

* The App will continue functioning normally.
* Advertisements may become less personalized.

You can change this permission anytime in:

**Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**
